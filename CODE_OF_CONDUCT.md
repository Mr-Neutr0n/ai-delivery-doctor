# Code of Conduct

AI Delivery Doctor welcomes technical discussion, bug reports, documentation improvements, reproducible field lessons, and code contributions from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Please:

- focus disagreement on code, evidence, and reproducible behavior;
- communicate respectfully;
- avoid publishing secrets, private customer data, or private deployment details;
- do not use Issues/PRs for harassment, spam, or unrelated promotion;
- disclose security-sensitive findings responsibly.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove content that puts users, contributors, or customer data at risk.

The goal is a practical engineering community where claims can be reviewed, reproduced, and improved.
